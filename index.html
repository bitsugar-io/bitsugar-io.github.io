What we do

We help teams put AI agents into real delivery work, run the platforms underneath, and build the apps on top.

Agents and AI

  • Agentic software delivery

    Coding agents that take work from ticket to pull request inside your existing SDLC. Tooling your whole team shares, and the same gates for every change, whether a person or an agent wrote it.

  • Verification loops

    Tests, evals and live checks wired into the loop, so agent output is proven before it merges and every failure feeds the next attempt.

  • Multi-agent systems

    Agents that coordinate over agent-to-agent (A2A) protocols, reach your tools through MCP with scoped access, and are measured on whether they get the job right.

Platforms and cloud

  • Cloud architecture

    Migrate, modernize, or build from scratch across AWS, GCP, and hybrid environments, with a strategy that avoids lock-in.

  • Platform engineering

    Internal developer platforms that remove friction, so your engineers ship features instead of fighting infrastructure.

  • DevOps and SRE

    Observability, incident response, and operational practices that keep your systems running.

  • Infrastructure automation

    Infrastructure as code, configuration management, and testing frameworks that make every deploy repeatable.

Apps

  • Mac and iOS apps

    Native Swift apps for Mac and iPhone, from menu bar utilities with deep system and Accessibility integration to App Store releases.
